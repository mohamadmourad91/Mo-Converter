## Description
Mo Converter lives next to your clock. Drag a file in Explorer, hold a key, and drop it on a segment of the wheel – the file is converted, compressed or edited right where it is.

Three wheels, three keys:
• Shift – Convert (PNG to WEBP, PDF to Word, MP4 to GIF, CSV to Excel …)
• Ctrl – Tools (compress, rotate, merge, arrange pages, read text …)
• Alt – Actions (copy path, show in Explorer, ZIP, read QR code …)

Your files never leave your computer. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

IMAGES – convert between PNG, JPG, WEBP, AVIF, TIFF, GIF, BMP and ICO. Compress, crop, rotate and adjust colours in a built-in editor. Hide faces and number plates. Remove metadata. Read text from pictures (OCR) and QR codes, create QR codes, make collages.

PDF – to Word, Excel (tables are detected), PowerPoint, Markdown, EPUB, text or images. Merge, split, rearrange and rotate pages, compress, and read scanned pages.

VIDEO AND AUDIO – MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, GIF, MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, OPUS. Compress, trim, rotate, change the speed, remove sound, even out the volume, save frames, split and join.

DOCUMENTS AND MORE – Word, Markdown, text, HTML, EPUB, CSV and Excel; with LibreOffice installed also PDF and OpenDocument. Unpack ZIP, 7z, RAR and TAR.GZ safely. Convert subtitles (SRT, VTT, ASS, SBV).

Results never overwrite your files. The interface is available in English, German and Arabic (right to left), in a light and a dark theme.
